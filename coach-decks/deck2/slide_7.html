Negatives close waste loops

  • A negative blocks the ad from showing for a term that cannot convert.
  • Negate clearly irrelevant terms such as wrong material, use, or product type.
  • Do not negate a relevant term merely because it has two or three clicks and no sale.
  • Choose negative phrase for a whole family; use negative exact when only one query is bad.
Decision Drill
Defend negative phrase, negative exact, lower bid, or watch.
