Fix the top of the funnel first

Low Impressions
Diagnostic Checks
  • Is the bid high enough to win auctions?
  • Is the campaign running out of budget early in the day?
  • Is the product properly indexed for the target keyword?
First Action
Raise bid incrementally, adjust daily budget pacing, or fix listing SEO.
Low CTR
Diagnostic Checks
  • Is the main image compelling and clear?
  • Is the price competitive on the search results page?
  • Does the title match the shopper's actual intent?
First Action
Improve the visual hook, adjust pricing, or negate irrelevant search terms.
