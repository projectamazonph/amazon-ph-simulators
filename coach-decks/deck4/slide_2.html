A useful report tells a story

  • Clients can see the dashboard; they pay you for the interpretation.
  • A data dump causes panic. A narrative builds trust.
  • Every report must state: What happened, Why it happened, and What we are doing next.
Reporting Comparison
Data Dump (Avoid) Narrative (Use)
"Spend was $500, sales were $1,000, ACOS is 50%." "ACOS hit 50% because we tested three new terms. We paused the losers and expect ACOS to drop next week."
"Impressions are down 20% this week." "Impressions dropped 20% as we lowered bids on broad match to protect profitability during the slow season."
