SOPs turn judgment into process

  • Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) prevent repeated mistakes.
  • A change log is your most important SOP: it records the why behind a decision.
  • If performance drops, the log tells you exactly what to reverse.
  • Good process allows a VA to act independently within safe boundaries.
Example Change Log
Date Action Rationale
Oct 12 Lowered bid on "bamboo board" to $0.65 ACOS hit 54%; reducing to find profitable floor.
Oct 14 Added negative exact "plastic board" 15 clicks, 0 sales. Clearly irrelevant material.
Oct 19 Raised budget on Exact Core by 20% Campaign capping at 2 PM with 22% ACOS.
