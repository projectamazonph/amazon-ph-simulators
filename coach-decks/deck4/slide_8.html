Client communication should be short and true

  • Clients read updates on phones — use BLUF.
  • Structure escalations: Situation, Impact, Recommendation.
  • Don't hide bad news; explain how you'll fix it.
  • Always bring a proposed solution, not just a question.
Avoid: The Rambling Update
Too long and vague: "ACOS is high today; not sure why. What should I do?"
Use: The Structured Escalation
Situation: ACOS spiked to 60% due to competitor bids on core terms.
Impact: Losing ~$20/day.
Recommendation: Lower bids by 15% on those terms to restore profitability. Approve?
