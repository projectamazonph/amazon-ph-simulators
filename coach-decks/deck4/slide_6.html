Permission protects the client

  • The permission ladder protects the client's budget and the VA's job.
  • Independent actions are low-risk, reversible, and reduce waste.
  • Escalations involve budget increases or major strategy shifts.
  • When escalating, always bring a proposed solution, not just a problem.
