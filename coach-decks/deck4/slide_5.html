Cadence prevents chaos

Daily
  • Check spend pacing and budget exhaustion
  • Monitor account alerts and suspensions
  • Verify inventory stock levels
  • Fix accidental pauses or errors
Weekly
  • Harvest search terms (winners and wasters)
  • Adjust bids based on statistical evidence
  • Add negative keywords to stop leaks
  • Send narrative client report
Monthly
  • Review overall campaign structure
  • Audit the top 20% of keywords driving 80% of sales
  • Review listing conversion rates
  • Archive dead or obsolete campaigns
