The capstone is a sequence

  • The capstone project simulates a complete client engagement.
  • It is not a single test, but a sequence of dependent decisions.
  • You cannot optimize what you haven't structured, and you cannot structure what you haven't researched.
  • Every stage requires documented evidence before moving to the next.
Capstone Workflow
Stage Required Evidence
1. Research 15-term intent list
2. Structure Campaign map & budget split
3. Launch Initial bids & match types
4. Optimize Change log & harvest table
5. Report Client narrative & next steps
