# PPC Coach Retrieval Practice Bank

## How to use

Use these prompts at the beginning or end of a lesson. Require a one- or two-sentence answer with evidence or a formula where relevant.

| Module | Retrieval prompt | What a strong answer includes |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Why can more traffic fail to produce more sales? | Buyability, offer, and listing conversion logic. |
| 1 | What happens between a shopper query and an order? | Funnel sequence and a possible break point. |
| 2 | What does ACOS measure, and what must it be compared against? | Spend / sales and margin or target guardrail. |
| 3 | Why separate campaign purposes? | Control, readability, and cleaner actions. |
| 4 | What is the difference between a keyword and search term? | Instruction versus shopper evidence. |
| 5 | What is the troubleshooting order before PPC? | Stock, price, reviews, competitors, PPC. |
| 6 | What must every launch plan include? | Purpose, guardrail, owner, evidence, review date. |
| 7 | What makes a bid change controlled? | Baseline, one variable, guardrail, review date. |
| 8 | Name the four harvest classifications. | Graduate, Negative, Lower / repair, Watch. |
| 9 | Why change one variable at a time? | Preserves causal learning. |
| 10 | What turns a metric update into a useful report? | Result, driver, action, guardrail, checkpoint. |
| 11 | When should a VA escalate? | Permission or risk outside agreed authority. |

## Simulator-workflow lab prompts (new lab lessons)

Use these after the learner finishes the matching lab lesson and before opening the simulator. Same standard: one or two sentences with evidence.

| Lab lesson | Retrieval prompt | What a strong answer includes |
|---|---|---|
| m1l6 BuyBox Dojo | Why does the same campaign convert differently on a repaired listing? | Readiness rows drive conversion; PPC numbers are listing numbers. |
| m3l5 Campaign Architect | A brief is missing the product's material. What comes before any campaign choice? | Ask for a better brief; never build on invented facts. |
| m4l5 SQP Studio | For a query with real demand but near-zero brand impressions, which read failed and what is the action? | Visibility failed; expand campaign coverage. |
| m4l6 Keyword Lab | Where does the maximum bid come from? | Margin and target ACOS, never the competitor's bid. |
| m5l5 Search Term Triage | A relevant term shows 25 clicks and 0 orders. What do you do? | Diagnose query, listing, price, offer; keep or lower bid, never auto-negative. |
| m5l6 AdConsole Pro | What do you do before opening the console each morning? | Write the decision list from yesterday's report first. |
| m8l7 Pacing Deck | A campaign caps at 2 PM at 55% ACOS against a 40% target. Budget action? | Fix efficiency first; the budget is protecting you from waste. |
| m8l8 Bid Decisions | A target has 95 clicks, 3 orders, 76% ACOS vs 40% target. Action and new bid from $1.45? | Lower 15% to $1.23; ACOS above target means cheaper, not bigger. |
| m9l7 Bulk File | A term shows 26 clicks and 3 orders. What row do you write? | Keyword add (harvest to exact) with target and bid; never a negative. |
| m10l6 Account Audit | 170 clicks, 1 order, 0.6% CVR against a 4% norm. Which quality failed? | Offer/listing quality; the ads delivered the shoppers. |
| m12l7 Client Onboarding | The client says "do whatever you think is best." What is missing? | A change-approval policy; propose it before changing anything. |
