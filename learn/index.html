Ano ba ang PPC Coach?

Teaching companion para sa Amazon PPC. Labingdalawang plain-language modules, 25-term glossary, search-term grader, campaign builder na nag-sscore ng work mo, one-page report generator, quiz arena na may 13 quizzes, AI coach, at teacher cohort view. Progress, levels, at badges naka-store sa browser mo.

Ginawa para sa Filipino VAs at junior strategists na pumapasok sa Amazon advertising roles. Walang fluff, walang jargon, walang theory na walang example.
